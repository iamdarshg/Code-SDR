## Changes incorporated

The schematic now includes the TPS7A8300 analog regulator and its correct pin
mapping, feedback and bypass network; five rail supervisors; corrected reset,
boot and crystal connections; corrected ADC/FPGA/PHY bypass placement; corrected
RF component land patterns; and QPC6144 control dividers. The candidate preserves
instance identity while integrating package and placement changes. Old tracks
that touched the wrong corrected lands were explicitly removed with receipts;
their required connections still need routing.

The final model specifies C11 as Murata GRM31CR71A226KE15L (22 uF, 10 V, X7R,
1206), C12/C346 as GRM32ER71A476KE15L (47 uF, 10 V, X7R, 1210), and FB33 as
BLM21SN300SN1D. These supersede older report references to X5R 1206 output
capacitors or a different core bead. Preserve the chosen parts' effective
capacitance, voltage, temperature and DCR requirements when substituting.

## 10 MHz–10 GHz option

The frequency plan retains the high-band path through 10 GHz and the optional
passive low-band path down to 10 MHz. The baseline low-band assembly fits C208
and C210 and omits R153/R156. The HF option fits R153/R156, omits C208/C210,
and disables U50. R153 and R156 are native DNP in the baseline schematic/model.
LO2 is 2400 MHz and IF1 is 2426.25 MHz. Detailed calculations and limitations
are in `../redesign/RF_ANALYSIS.md` and the recovery mixer/frequency reviews.

Manufacturer review supports the unconventional ADL5801 upconversion and LT5560
mixer configurations; they were not rejected solely for being unconventional.
This preserves design optionality, not measured sensitivity, image rejection or
controlled-impedance performance over the full range.
