## Added acceptance requirement

Preserve optional 10 MHz–10 GHz reception. The existing GRF4001 low-band
population is specified from 100 MHz. The planned passive HF population fits
R153/R156, omits C208/C210 and disables U50; both bypass endpoint links are DNP
in the amplified population. It retains the DC–2.5 GHz low-pass filter and the
existing high-band paths. Coverage, conversion images and population-specific
gain/noise limits require explicit documentation and validation.
